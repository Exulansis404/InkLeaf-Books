## What it does

- **Reads almost anything:** EPUB, PDF, TXT, FB2, HTML, Markdown and comics (CBZ/CBR).
- **Easy on the eyes:** e-ink style pages, good fonts, light and dark themes, and seven page-turn styles.
- **Make it yours:** highlight, underline, scribble anywhere with finger or stylus, add stickers, and keep as many bookmarks as you like.
- **Free bookstore:** thousands of free classics from Project Gutenberg, plus free Bangla books from Bangla Wikisource and the Internet Archive. Read them straight away.
- **Writing desk:** write your own stories and books with chapters, notes and character sheets, read them like any other book, and export them as EPUB.
- **Read aloud, dictionary and reading stats.**
- **Optional backup to your own Google Drive:** your settings, reading places, notes and writing come back on a new phone.

## How to install

1. On your Android phone or tablet, tap **[Download InkLeaf](https://github.com/Exulansis404/InkLeaf-Books/releases/latest/download/InkLeaf.apk)**.
2. Open the downloaded **InkLeaf.apk**.
3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**, then tap **Install**.

## Updates

InkLeaf checks for new versions by itself. When one is ready, it asks you, downloads it and installs it with one tap. Your books, notes and settings stay as they are. You can also check any time from the Library's **⋮** menu → **Check for updates**.
