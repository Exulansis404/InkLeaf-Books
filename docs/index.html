Your own backupOptional backup to your own Google Drive, so a new phone gets everything back.

How to install

  1. On your Android phone or tablet, tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded InkLeaf.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source, then tap Install.

After that, InkLeaf keeps itself up to date: when a new version is ready it asks you, and installs it with one tap.
